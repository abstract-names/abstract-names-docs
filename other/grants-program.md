# Grants Program

The Grants Program supports integrations, tooling, and ecosystem contributions that adopt Abstract Names (ANS). Builders integrating with Abstract Names may be eligible for funding based on published criteria, impact, and milestones.

### What we fund

We support targeted, integration‑oriented work that strengthens the Abstract Names ecosystem:

* App integrations that improve UX with name resolution and profiles.
* Tooling such as SDKs, indexers, React hooks, and developer documentation.
* Community efforts including tutorials, workshops, and translations.

Full end‑to‑end dapp builds are out of scope.

### Funding model

Awards are milestone‑based and tied to measurable impact (for example, active integrations, user adoption, or performance improvements). Funding decisions consider alignment with the Abstract Names roadmap, technical quality, and the expected benefit to users and developers.

### How to apply

Submit a short proposal that outlines:

* Scope, milestones, and timeline.
* Expected impact and how it will be measured.
* Public repositories or portfolio links.

### Evaluation criteria

Proposals are evaluated on:

* User impact and ecosystem value.
* Technical quality and feasibility.
* Clarity of milestones and delivery plan.

Accepted proposals will receive scoped grants and support from the team.

### Disclaimer

We may decline any request at our discretion. Eligibility, criteria, and award guidelines are subject to change without notice.
