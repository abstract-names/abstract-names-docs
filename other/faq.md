# FAQ

<details>

<summary><strong>What is Abstract Names?</strong></summary>

A naming system for Abstract global wallets that maps readable names to addresses, profiles, and content.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>How do I get a name?</strong></summary>

Go to [abstractnames.xyz](https://app.gitbook.com/u/D5ma2oQFF5URXTbXmtprrywY8yg2) to mint, register, and set your profile. Minting follows phased rules (whitelist → waitlist → public).

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Do names expire?</strong></summary>

Yes. Renew annually (or multiple years) via the Controller. Expired names stop resolving after grace periods.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>What characters are allowed?</strong></summary>

ASCII a–z, 0–9, hyphen, underscore, and selected Unicode (CJK, Hangul, Hiragana, Katakana, Latin extensions). Length: 3–63.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Can I transfer my name?</strong></summary>

Yes, it’s an ERC‑721 NFT. On transfer, resolver clears address/contenthash and invalidates old text records for safety.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>How does reverse resolution work?</strong></summary>

Set a primary name (small fee) to map address → name.abs. Apps can display your name instead of your address.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Where can I see contracts?</strong></summary>

See Deployments for addresses and ABScan links once available.

</details>
