# Abstract Basics

## What is Abstract?

Abstract is a [Layer 2](https://ethereum.org/en/layer-2/) (L2) network built on top of [Ethereum](https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/), designed to securely power consumer-facing blockchain applications at scale with low fees and fast transaction speeds. Instead of creating a new wallet everywhere, you keep one global wallet. Apps can simplify the technical steps in the background, so everyday actions feel familiar and fast.

Your global wallet is the anchor for your on‑chain identity. It holds your assets, but it can also be recognized by a readable name. When you attach an Abstract Name to your wallet, people can send to “yourname” instead of a long address, and apps can display a profile with links and an avatar.

### Getting started

Getting started is simple: create or connect a wallet using the Abstract onboarding flow, which establishes your global identity. Once you’re set up, you can claim an Abstract Name and decide what it should point to... your receiving address, a profile, or a piece of content.

Using a name makes interactions clearer. Friends and apps can find you by name, and with reverse resolution your address can display as your chosen name in supported interfaces. Profiles add context with text records like a website or social handle, helping others confirm they’re looking at the right account.

For more about the Abstract platform, visit the official documentation at [docs.abs.xyz.](https://docs.abs.xyz/overview)
